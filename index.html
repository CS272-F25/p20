The Reading Room

The Reading Room is a curated space where the designers of The Next Chapter recommend books they personally love. These selections reflect the stories that inspired us, stayed with us, and deserve a special spotlight.

Not Quite Dead Yet by Holly Jackson

A fast-paced mystery packed with secrets that refuse to stay buried.

Why we love it: Clever twists and nonstop tension.

Never Lie by Freida McFadden

Newlyweds trapped by a snowstorm uncover disturbing secrets.

Why we love it: Claustrophobic and impossible to put down.

The Secret Book Society by Madeline Martin

WWII-era London women form a hidden book club as an act of resistance.

Why we love it: A heartfelt tribute to readers and community.
