Atmosphere by Taylor Jenkins Reid

Set against the electric world of 1980s NASA, following astronauts as ambition, love, and sacrifice collide beneath the vastness of space.

Why we love it: Emotional intimacy meets cosmic wonder.

Not Quite Dead Yet by Holly Jackson

A fast-paced mystery packed with secrets that refuse to stay buried.

Why we love it: Clever twists and nonstop tension.

Never Lie by Freida McFadden

Newlyweds trapped by a snowstorm uncover disturbing secrets hidden in a remote house.

Why we love it: Claustrophobic and impossible to put down.

The Secret Book Society by Madeline Martin

WWII-era London women form a hidden book club that becomes an act of resistance.

Why we love it: A love letter to readers and community.
